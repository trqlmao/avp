# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a technical project and a welcoming one. Everyone interacting in the issues, pull requests, and
discussions is expected to be respectful and to assume good faith. We want contributions from many
different clients and communities, so keep the focus on the work.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Keeping discussion technical and on topic.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official channel)
and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers via GitHub: open the repository's **Security**
tab and choose **Report a vulnerability** (it doubles as a private contact channel), or use a private
report to a maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments,
contributions, or contributors that violate this code.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
